# Bildvorlagen für Kirchende, Revision 2

Erzeugt mit dem integrierten Bildgenerator. Referenzen: die vom Nutzer bereitgestellten Ansichten des realen Kirchender Denkmals und seines Bodens. Originale bleiben in der Bildgenerierung erhalten; im Spiel werden optimierte WebP-Dateien verwendet.

## Denkmal
One isolated game-ready 2.5D sprite of the actual Kirchende memorial from reference photographs, transparent RGBA background; restrained slightly elevated three-quarter camera, full silhouette. Tall rectangular rough gray-brown rubble-stone pier, pale weathered mortar, low two-course base, thin overhanging square cap and small rounded stone helmet. Front plaque: “UNSERN HELDEN”, small square cross relief underneath; adjacent pale sandstone roll-of-honor panel with worn engraving strokes and no invented legible names. Subtle moss and damp staining, diffuse natural lighting, readable at 180–240 pixels tall. No gilding, glow, fantasy embellishments, ground plane, surrounding props or interface.

## Boden
Square seamless-style top-down terrain albedo inspired by the reference ground: muted green moss and sparse short grass dominate, compacted gray-brown earth and scattered small dry oak/maple leaf fragments, fine grit and thin twig fragments. Orthographic diffuse illumination, edge-to-edge material, no central focal object, perspective, shadows, paving, buildings, people or text. The renderer repeats the optimized image at a fixed 512-world-pixel tile scale; exact seamlessness is not guaranteed by image generation.
